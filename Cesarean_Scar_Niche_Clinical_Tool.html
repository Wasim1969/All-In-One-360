Clinical Assessment & Recommended Management

Evidence & important distinctions

  • A niche is an indentation at a previous cesarean scar; an ultrasound finding alone does not establish symptomatic cesarean scar disorder.
  • 2025 SOGC consensus: asymptomatic niches generally need no treatment; decisions depend on symptoms, RMT and fertility plans.
  • Hysteroscopic remodeling may be considered for symptomatic patients with RMT ≥3 mm; repair via laparoscopic/vaginal approaches may be considered for symptomatic fertility-seeking patients, especially RMT <3 mm. These are conditional options, not automatic indications.
  • Cesarean scar ectopic pregnancy is a separate high-risk pregnancy diagnosis and should not follow routine niche management.

SOGC Clinical Consensus No. 463 (2025) · JAMA Network Open: Cesarean Scar Disorder Definition (2023) · SMFM Consult #63: Cesarean Scar Ectopic Pregnancy
