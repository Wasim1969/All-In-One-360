Clinical Assessment & Recommended Management

Clinical sources and safety note

Decision support prototype based primarily on NICE NG126 — Ectopic pregnancy and miscarriage, especially diagnostic review, expectant management, methotrexate and surgery. See also ACOG — Ectopic Pregnancy. Non-tubal ectopic sites require specialist site-specific pathways. This tool does not replace urgent obstetric evaluation or establish insurance coverage. Missing values are unknown, never assumed normal.
